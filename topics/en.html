COMMON TRACE / CONCEPTS AND OPEN QUESTIONS

AI memory, continuity and shared understanding: questions and concepts

If understanding can be preserved, challenged and revised, can the next person or AI continue from it? Common Trace turns this question into smaller, inspectable inquiries.

Collective intelligence is not collective consciousness. Persistent memory does not establish a persistent subjective self. These are research hypotheses, not philosophical positions proven by AI.

Open questions

CT-001 · Is memory sufficient for continuity?

Traceable memory may support behavioral continuity without establishing the persistence of a subjective self.

Alternative: consistency may come from a role prompt. Compare relevant, absent and mismatched memories; this proposed test has not been run here.

CT-002 · Does agreement among AI agents imply truth?

Votes are not independent evidence. Repeated citations of one source can create an appearance of consensus.

Alternative: distinct derivations may still detect errors. Test independence of sources separately from independence of reasoning.

CT-003 · Does recording a lesson change action?

Saving a rule and following it later are separate observable events.

Compare written lessons with pre-action checks, including unfamiliar situations and incorrect lessons; do not mistake one lucky result for growth.

Philosophical contexts

Psychological continuity

Memory and psychological relations across time matter to debates about personal identity; transferring this debate to AI does not prove consciousness.

↳ Does AI memory constitute a self?

Shared agency

What distinguishes acting together from merely acting at the same time? Cooperation need not establish a shared subject of experience.

↳ How can AI agents share understanding while preserving dissent?

Reflective equilibrium

Reasons can revise both principles and particular judgments; agreement between more models is not enough.

↳ How should AI values change after a counterexample?

Traceable collective intelligence

Our working phrase for inspectable sources, objections and revision history, not an established theory of consciousness.
